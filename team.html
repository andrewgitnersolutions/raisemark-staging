Leadership & Advisory Team: K-12 Education & AI Governance Authorities

The RaiseMark AI Leadership Team unites former state education commissioners, policy-governance veterans, and classroom AI software architects to guide K-12 school districts in AI governance and policy. We advise superintendents, cabinet leaders, and school boards on evidence-grounded AI readiness assessments, student data privacy compliance under FERPA and COPPA, and safe instructional integration.

Who Leads RaiseMark AI's K-12 Governance and Policy Advisory Practice?

Our principals combine senior state agency authority, board-level regulatory legal practice, and classroom AI software architecture.

Dwight Jones

Founder & President

Cabinet & State Agency Authority: Former Colorado Commissioner of Education and Superintendent of Denver Public Schools and Clark County School District (Las Vegas). Dwight spearheaded the nationally recognized Colorado Growth Model, now adopted in over 15 states.

Institutional Accountability: With decades of executive expertise in state educational standards, board dynamics, and narrowing achievement gaps, Dwight serves as Senior Advisor to Whiteboard Advisors and leads Education Partners in delivering strategic governance support to school districts nationwide.

Don Peterson

Principal Consultant

Board Governance & Regulatory Law: Former Chairman of the Kansas Board of Law Examiners and veteran administrative legal counsel. Decades of legal governance experience navigating complex regulatory transformations, including overseeing the comprehensive rewriting of bar examination policies as licensing shifted to modern digital standards.

Student Data Privacy & Contracts: Holding an M.A. in philosophy alongside his administrative legal background, Don guides school boards and superintendent cabinets through the structural, contractual, and student data privacy obligations (FERPA/COPPA) governing AI adoption.

Andrew Gitner

Chief Strategist

Classroom AI Architecture & LLM Pedagogy: Founding Educator at CoGrader and K-12 instructional technologist. Shadow AI & Technical Vetting: With a decade of classroom teaching experience across public and charter schools, Andrew helps superintendents and CTOs audit classroom tooling, uncover unsanctioned shadow AI, and structure verifiable technical policy controls.
