The RaiseMark Methodology

Compass. Map. Path.

The RaiseMark AI Methodology (Compass, Map, Path) is a structured, three-stage governance framework engineered for K-12 superintendents, district cabinets, and school boards. A superintendent cannot recommend what they cannot name: our framework provides an actionable pathway from discovering unvetted shadow AI to adopting board-approved acceptable use policies (AUP) and maintaining continuous statutory compliance.

How Do RaiseMark AI's Three Governance Stages Protect School Districts?

Each engagement serves a specific purpose, answers an explicit leadership question, and establishes clear institutional boundaries.

Stage 01 · See

Stage 01 (Compass): How Do Districts Inventory Classroom AI and Detect Shadow Tools?

The Question It Answers: “What AI tools are currently inside our district, who is using them, and where is our student data exposed?”

Before a district can regulate or purchase AI, leadership must establish baseline visibility. The Compass audits your current digital environment, identifying approved enterprise tools alongside unsanctioned browser extensions and mobile app-store utilities that staff and students already reach.

What Your District Receives

  • Reconciled Software Inventory: Comprehensive digital ledger of student-reachable AI tools.
  • Shadow AI Detection: Diagnostic discovery across browser, mobile, and desktop endpoints.
  • Multi-Dimensional Risk Scoring: Proprietary evaluation across instructional efficacy, FERPA/COPPA compliance, and student data privacy alignment.
  • Prioritized Risk Mitigation List: Documented 90-day immediate action plan for district cabinets.

What It Does Not Cover

The Snapshot is strictly an empirical diagnostic instrument. It does not draft board policy, rewrite acceptable use agreements, or conduct staff-wide professional development.

Stage 02 · Decide · Flagship

Stage 02 (Map): How Do School Boards Develop Enforceable K-12 AI Acceptable Use Policies?

The Question It Answers: “What are our district's rules of the road, and how do we protect student privacy while empowering teachers?”

Taking the diagnostic findings as an empirical foundation, the Map guides superintendent cabinets through 4 structured visioning sessions to establish institutional consensus and draft board-ready policy tiered by grade band.

What Your District Receives

  • Facilitated Cabinet Alignment: 4 executive visioning and consensus-building sessions.
  • Board-Ready AI Policy Framework: Custom Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) tiered by grade band (K-5, 6-8, 9-12).
  • Vendor Procurement Governance: Data Privacy Agreement (DPA) AI addenda and contractual model training prohibitions.
  • Community Rollout Packet: Clear communication guidelines for families, educators, and school board members.

What It Does Not Cover

The Map establishes policy and procurement rules. It does not implement technical firewall blocking rules, reconfigure network infrastructure, or replace your district's legal counsel.

Our Positioning & Philosophy

Why Does RaiseMark AI Maintain Strict Independence Between Audits, Policy Writing, and Vendor Retainers?

Many consulting vendors bundle audits, template policies, staff workshops, and roadmaps into a single rushed 90-day package. The result is almost always a generic, rubber-stamped policy document that fails to reflect actual classroom practice or technical reality.

RaiseMark maintains a deliberate, disciplined separation between each stage. A diagnostic is not policy writing; it is an empirical investigation. Policy development must take real audit data as its foundation, not guesswork. And ongoing retainer support exists to sustain what leadership has established, rather than to sell perpetual pre-packaged services.

This structural separation ensures your district receives rigorous, uncompromised analysis at every milestone — with zero obligation to buy the next stage until you are ready.
