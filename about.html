Our Philosophy: Evidence-Based AI Governance for K-12 Districts

RaiseMark AI is an independent educational consulting firm specializing in AI governance, policy development, and readiness diagnostics for K-12 school districts. Founded by former state education commissioners, school attorneys, and classroom AI researchers, RaiseMark equips superintendents and school boards to govern emerging technology while safeguarding student data privacy under FERPA and COPPA.

What Core Principles Govern Safe K-12 AI Adoption?

Our institutional framework translates educational values, legal compliance, and technological rigor into actionable school district governance.

Evidence-Based

Data Over Speculation: We rely on empirical data and proven methodologies. Every recommendation is backed by technical research, tested in practice, and evaluated against objective safety benchmarks. The divide between AI claims and classroom reality can only be bridged with empirical telemetry, not speculation.

Cross-Disciplinary

Holistic Integration: Comprehensive K-12 AI governance requires more than IT configuration. We unite executive cabinet leadership, school board legal practice, and classroom AI architecture to align policy, pedagogy, student data privacy, and district operations.

Values-Led Approach

Ethical Alignment: Classroom AI tools are audited directly against your community's institutional values. We help superintendent cabinets and school boards translate educational standards into enforceable Acceptable Use Policies and transparent ethical guardrails.

Human-Focused

Augmenting Educators: Technology must augment human capability, not supplant it. We focus on instructional tools and administrative workflows that empower teachers and staff, ensuring AI remains subordinate to human judgment and student wellbeing.

Partnership-Driven

Multi-Year Stewardship: When we advise a district, we invest in leadership's long-term success. Our advisory relationship extends beyond delivering static reports; we provide continuous strategic alignment to ensure policies adapt as generative AI models evolve.

Process-Centered

Operational Excellence: We examine and refine district operational workflows from software procurement to classroom deployment, ensuring AI integration is sustainable, compliant with state statutes, and protective of student records.

How Does RaiseMark AI Guide School Districts Through AI Policy and Readiness Assessments?

Our engagement model is engineered for depth, institutional clarity, and lasting compliance. We collaborate directly with your cabinet to design solutions that protect student privacy.

Assess: AI Readiness & Shadow AI Audit

Baseline Visibility: We evaluate active software, network logs, and instructional workflows through an AI Readiness Audit—identifying sanctioned enterprise tools alongside unsanctioned shadow AI and privacy exposures.

Design: Board-Ready Policy & Roadmap

Tailored Governance: We guide district cabinets through structured visioning sessions to author enforceable Acceptable Use Policies (AUP) tiered by grade band (K-5, 6-8, 9-12) and vendor procurement guidelines.

Implement: Capability & Staff Alignment

Systemic Execution: We work directly with cabinet leaders, curriculum directors, and technology teams to establish clear approval workflows, vetting procedures, and administrative oversight.

Measure: Continuous Compliance & KPIs

Accountable Governance: We establish measurable evaluation criteria from day one, tracking statutory compliance, vendor data privacy agreement (DPA) adherence, and instructional effectiveness.
